# Reception-Assisted Admission

## Goal

A receptionist can admit a patient identified by DNI, collect a structured clinical form, obtain a non-editable triage priority, and place the consultation in the same dynamic `EntradaCola` used by digital patients. This flow does not create or use `Chat`.

## Domain

- `Paciente.usuarioAuth` is optional for reception-created patients.
- Patient identity fields are stored on `Paciente`; `OrigenRegistroPaciente.RECEPCION` identifies local registrations.
- Existing digital patients are reused by matching normalized DNI against `Paciente.numeroDocumento` or `UsuarioAuth.numeroDocumento`.
- `SesionRecepcion` selects one assigned hospital. A receptionist may work in several hospitals but can have only one active session.
- `AdmisionRecepcion` stores the structured form and resulting triage JSON for audit.
- `ConsultaMedica.codigoLlamado` is an anonymous public code for the hospital screen.
- `IngresoColaService` is the shared entry point for both digital and reception flows.

## Flow

1. List hospitals assigned to the authenticated receptionist.
2. Resume the active reception session or start one for a hospital.
3. Search by normalized DNI.
4. Create an admission, reusing or creating the patient.
5. Complete the whole structured form.
6. Finalize once. `TriageFormularioService` classifies the form in one AI call, without conversation.
7. The receptionist cannot submit or modify priority.
8. The admission, consultation, and queue entry are finalized transactionally.
9. The response includes `codigoLlamado` and dynamic estimation.

## Rules

- DNI is required and normalized to digits.
- Emergencies/NN patients are outside this administrative flow.
- A patient cannot have two active consultations.
- A receptionist cannot have two active sessions.
- A reception session cannot close with unfinished admissions.
- The hospital comes from the active session, never from the admission request.
- Specialty must belong to that hospital.
- Priority is generated by the backend and is not editable by reception.
- If the AI provider fails, a conservative deterministic fallback classifies explicit alarm signs and severe pain.
- `EntradaCola` remains the queue source of truth.

## Endpoints

```http
GET /api/recepcion/hospitales
GET /api/recepcion/sesiones/activa
POST /api/recepcion/sesiones
POST /api/recepcion/sesiones/{sesionId}/cerrar
GET /api/recepcion/pacientes/{dni}?sesionId={sesionId}
POST /api/recepcion/admisiones
POST /api/recepcion/admisiones/{admisionId}/finalizar
```
